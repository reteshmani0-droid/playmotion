HOW TO PLAY

Slice. Combo. Don't hit bombs.

Swipe fast

Move your hand quickly through fruit. Slow movement doesn't cut — speed is everything.

Chain combos

Cut several fruit in one swipe for combo multipliers. Three or more gets loud.

Avoid bombs

One bomb ends the run instantly. Let them fall — they don't cost you a life.

3 misses = over

Dropped fruit costs a life. Bombs are instant. Keep both hands moving.

Setup: TV and phone on the same Wi-Fi. Phone 2–3 m back at chest height, facing you. Stand where the camera sees both hands.
